Rules

  • White runs clockwise, Black anticlockwise, round and round.
  • The gate at the top is the seam where the board's two ends were joined — both of you cross it, in opposite directions.
  • Cross it and the checker is crowned: it lands on the far side, can no longer be hit, and holds its point alone.
  • Hit a lone uncrowned checker and it goes to the bar, owing a full lap of 24 pips again.
  • Crown all fifteen to win.
